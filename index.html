About

A young company with a rich heritage

VEZ Farmachem inherited decades of business experience in the generic pharmaceutical market. We took part in the growth of the entire Farmachem Group, and since the 2014 restructuring a substantial part of the activities of the former Swiss headquarters is run from Zagreb, inside the European Union.

We work in procurement of new technologies, co-development and distribution of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients, cooperating exclusively with highly respectable pharmaceutical producers.

  1. 2001

    Representative office in Kyiv

    Opened as part of Farmachem Machineries and Marketing S.A., starting cooperation with all major Ukrainian pharmaceutical companies.

  2. 2014

    Headquarters move to Croatia

    Group restructuring transfers a substantial part of activities from Switzerland to VEZ Farmachem d.o.o. in Zagreb.

  3. 2017

    HALMED GDP approval

    The Croatian Agency for Medicinal Products and Medical Devices confirms compliance with EU Good Distribution Practice.

What we do

Expert sourcing, from active substance to finished form

01

API sourcing

More than 30 years of collaboration with API producers of every kind — small, large, institutional or private — lets us deliver sourcing solutions tailored to individual requirements, exclusively from reliable, reputable, high-quality manufacturers.

02

In-bulk finished dosage forms

Many producers now offer finished dosage forms alongside API. Partners can start development from scratch or select an already developed, ready-made form in bulk — saving valuable time and development effort.

03

Agency representation

Years of collaboration built a strong bond of trust with the generic industry. Producers that are global leaders in their fields approach VEZ Farmachem to represent them in the territories where we are active.

04

Regulatory know-how

We know the regulatory processes of our territories well and can anticipate what customers need and what producers must prepare in advance. The result is a faster product launch with minimal obstacles and delays.

Added value

Present on both sides of the market

Our work acts as a catalyst. Broad knowledge of producers, their capabilities and their specific strengths lets us suggest the right partner from the beginning — and connect principals with clients so transparently that they soon become long-term partners.

Quality and compliance

Regulated by European GDP

VEZ Farmachem d.o.o. operates in compliance with EU GMP Volume 4, Part II, Chapter 17, and the Guidelines of 19 March 2015 on principles of Good Distribution Practice of active substances for medicinal products for human use.

  • Documented quality management system

    Our QMS governs the processes, procedures and responsibilities behind our quality policies and objectives.

  • 23 standard operating procedures

    Approved SOPs cover both Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients and Medical Devices.

  • Traceability, end to end

    A maintained system of traceability, with every quality-related procedure described and approved.

  • Audit-ready documentation

    All QMS documentation is prepared and readily available for inspection during audit.

HALMED approval No. 381-10-05/228-17-05 · 24 October 2017
